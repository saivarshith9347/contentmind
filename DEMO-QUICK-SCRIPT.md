## 🚨 If Things Go Wrong

**Animation freezes**:
> "The AI is thinking harder than usual - that's 335 memories being processed!"

**Wrong step shown**:
- Click Previous to go back
- Stay calm, narrate what you're doing

**Complete failure**:
> "Let me show you the architecture instead" → Talk through design while reloading
